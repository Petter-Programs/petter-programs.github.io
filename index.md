# Petter Projects

Small games and tools for Android.

## Mosaical Pop

A colorful block puzzle: fill rows and columns to clear them, and match nine blocks of one color in a 3×3 square to pop them - along with every block of that color touching it.

- [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.petterprojects.mosaicalpop)
- [Privacy policy](https://petter-programs.github.io/privacy-policies/mosaical-pop/)
